### Hire Purchase

**Hire purchase (HP)** is buying on credit: you pay a **deposit** now and then equal **monthly instalments** over a fixed time. Interest (usually simple interest) is charged on the amount borrowed. HP almost always costs more than paying cash.

**Formulas**
- Balance financed = cash price − deposit
- Interest = balance × r × t (simple interest)
- Amount to repay = balance + interest
- Monthly instalment = amount to repay ÷ number of months
- Total HP cost = deposit + (instalment × number of months)
- Interest charged = total HP cost − cash price

**Worked example.** Cash price R8 000, deposit 10%, simple interest 18% per year on the balance over 2 years.
Deposit = 0,10 × 8 000 = R800. Balance = 8 000 − 800 = R7 200.
Interest = 7 200 × 0,18 × 2 = R2 592. Repay = 7 200 + 2 592 = R9 792.
Months = 24, so instalment = 9 792 ÷ 24 = R408.
Total HP cost = 800 + 9 792 = R10 592, which is R2 592 (the interest) more than the cash price.

**Why interest is on the balance.** You pay the deposit immediately, so you only borrow the balance. Interest is the cost of borrowing, so it applies to that balance, not the full cash price.

**Pitfalls**
- Forgetting to add the deposit when finding the total HP cost.
- Charging interest on the full cash price instead of on the balance after the deposit.
- Not converting the time period to years for the simple-interest step, or the period to months for the instalment count.
